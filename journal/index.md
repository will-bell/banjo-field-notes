# Journal

This is the messy part.

The point here is to record what I think I understand **before** it gets cleaned up. Entries can include wrong ideas, questions, audio clips, diagrams, experiments, and later corrections.

## Entry format

A useful pattern:

1. What I think is true
2. Why I think it is true
3. What I tried on the banjo
4. What confused me
5. What changed afterward
