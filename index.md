# Banjo Field Notes

A running notebook for learning banjo, music theory, tunings, songs, and whatever else I figure out along the way.

This site is intentionally simple. The goal is to preserve what I think I understand, test it against the instrument, and leave a trail of how that understanding changes over time.

## Current focus

- Open G tuning: `gDGBD`
- Double C tuning: `gCGCD`
- Chord construction
- Scale degrees and I-IV-V harmony
- Connecting what I hear to what I see on the fretboard

## Why this exists

I want a place where I can write down my own understanding before it gets polished away. Some notes will be wrong. That's useful.

The journal is the record of learning. The reference pages are the cleaner version that emerges afterward.
